Equation of a Circle

Day 29

Today you will learn to read a circle’s equation to find its center and radius, then write the equation yourself.

(x − h)² + (y − k)² = r²

Center at the origin

x² + y² = r²

Every point on a circle is the same distance, r, from the center. Here the center is (0, 0).

Take the point P(3, 4). Go 3 across and 4 up. You get a right triangle, and the radius is its longest side.

By the Pythagorean theorem: 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5². So r = 5.

Reading it: center at the origin

If you see x² + y² = a number, the center is (0, 0). The number is r², so take its square root.

Find the center and radius of

x² + y² = 25

r² = 25, so r = 5

Center (0, 0), radius 5 units

Find the center and radius of

x² + y² = 100

r² = 100, so r = 10

Center (0, 0), radius 10 units

Watch out: the right side is r², not r.

Center not at the origin

(x − h)² + (y − k)² = r²

The center is at (h, k). The radius still connects the center to a point P(x, y) on the circle.

h moves it left or right. k moves it up or down. r sets the size.

3 -2 4

Find the center and radius

x² + (y − 4)² = 16

Write as (x − 0)² + (y − 4)² = 4²

h = 0, k = 4, r = 4. Center (0, 4), radius 4 units.

(x + 1)² + (y − 7)² = 36

Write as (x − (−1))² + (y − 7)² = 6²

h = −1, k = 7, r = 6. Center (−1, 7), radius 6 units.

The signs flip: x + 1 means h = −1. (y − 7) means k = 7.

Write the equation

Center at (6, 7), contains the point (2, 5).

Step 1. Find the radius with the distance formula.

r = √[(2 − 6)² + (5 − 7)²]
r = √(16 + 4) = √20 = 2√5

Step 2. Substitute h = 6, k = 7, r = 2√5.

(x − 6)² + (y − 7)² = (2√5)²
(x − 6)² + (y − 7)² = 20

General form

Center at (−5, −4), radius 3 units. Write it as x² + y² + Ax + By + C = 0.

Substitute into (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r²

[x − (−5)]² + [y − (−4)]² = 3²

Simplify

(x + 5)² + (y + 4)² = 9

Expand by squaring each binomial

x² + 10x + 25 + y² + 8y + 16 = 9
x² + y² + 10x + 8y + 32 = 0
